1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is [registered entity name], [registered address] (“NegoAsia”, “we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or about your data, write to info@negoasia.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us. When you submit the contact form, book an audit, or correspond with us: your name, email address, telephone number, company, role, and whatever you choose to tell us about the negotiation you are facing.

Information collected during an engagement. Documents, figures and context shared with us so that we can do the work. This material is treated as confidential and is covered by section 9 of our Terms & Conditions.

Information collected automatically. When you browse this website: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, time spent, and the site that referred you. This is collected through analytics cookies and only with your consent — see section 9.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data as defined by the PDPA, and we ask that you do not send us any.

3. Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and organise the introductory audit.
  • To prepare, deliver and invoice our services.
  • To maintain our accounting and legal records.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To send occasional professional updates, where you have asked to receive them.

We do not sell personal data, we do not rent our contact list, and we do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Legal basis

Depending on the situation, we rely on: your consent (contact form, analytics cookies, professional updates); the performance of a contract or steps taken at your request before entering one; our legitimate interest in running and improving a professional practice, balanced against your rights; and compliance with legal obligations, in particular accounting and tax record-keeping.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us operate, and only to the extent they need it:

  • Netlify — website hosting and form submissions.
  • Google — website analytics, where you have consented.
  • Our email provider, accountant and, where relevant, our legal advisers.

Each of these is bound by confidentiality and data-protection obligations. We also disclose data where we are legally required to do so.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers are located outside Thailand, including in the European Union and the United States. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards permitted by the PDPA and, where the GDPR applies, on standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: 24 months from the last exchange, so that we can pick up a conversation you restart.
  • Client records and engagement files: for the duration of the relationship and 5 years afterwards.
  • Accounting and tax records: 5 years, as required by Thai accounting law.
  • Analytics data: 14 months.
  • Cookie consent choice: stored in your own browser until you clear it or change it from the footer.

After these periods the data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised.

8. Your rights

Under the PDPA — and under the GDPR where it applies to you — you have the right to: be informed about the processing; access a copy of your data; have inaccurate data corrected; ask for deletion; ask us to restrict or object to processing; receive your data in a portable format; and withdraw consent.

To exercise any of these, write to info@negoasia.com. We reply without undue delay and in any case within 30 days, as required by the PDPA. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Cookies

Cookies are small files placed on your device. This website uses:

  • Strictly necessary cookies, required for the site and its forms to work. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics 4, which tells us which pages are read and how visitors arrive. IP addresses are anonymised. These are set only if you accept them, and nothing from Google is loaded before you do.

There is no advertising cookie, no cross-site tracking and no data sold to anyone. Declining costs you nothing: the site behaves identically. You can change your mind at any time via Cookie settings at the bottom of every page, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

Embedded content — for example the YouTube video on the TEDx page — may set its own cookies when you play it. We use the privacy-enhanced YouTube domain, which does not set tracking cookies until playback starts.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to personal data is restricted to those who need it, and we apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed completely secure; please do not send highly confidential documents by email without agreeing a secure channel with us first.

11. Children

This website and our services are addressed to professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of twenty. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is the one published here, with its date at the top. Material changes will be signalled on the website.

13. Contact & complaints

Write to info@negoasia.com with any question or concern. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) in Thailand or, where the GDPR applies, with your local supervisory authority.
