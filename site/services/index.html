01 Coaching

Individual coaching

Acquire the mindset and the skills of professional negotiators, applied to the deals in front of you right now, not to a case study from a textbook.

Sessions alternate between method and live preparation: we work through your actual file, rehearse the difficult moments, then debrief what really happened at the table.

Format
One-to-one, in person in Bangkok or by video
Typical rhythm
Six to ten sessions over three months, plus support between sessions on live files
Languages
English and French
Who it's for
  • Founders and executives who negotiate deals large enough that a few points of margin change the year.
  • Senior sales and procurement leaders who close well but concede more than they should.
  • Anyone who has just been handed a negotiation they know they are not equipped for.
What changes
  • You walk in with a prepared position, alternatives, and a clear walk-away point instead of a hope.
  • You stop trading concessions and start trading conditions.
  • Pressure stops making your decisions for you.

02 Assistance

Negotiation assistance

(Re)gain control of a live, high-stakes negotiation with a world-class strategist at your side, before the next round rather than after the post-mortem.

We build the strategy with your team, prepare the scenarios and the concession ladder, and stay available between rounds. When it helps, Nicolas sits at the table with you; when it doesn't, he stays deliberately invisible.

Format
Strategy workshop, round-by-round preparation, optional presence at the table
Engaged
From a single decisive round to a full negotiation cycle
Confidentiality
Mutual NDA signed before any file is shared
Typical situations
  • A strategic renewal where the other side has just announced a double-digit demand.
  • A joint venture or distribution agreement where the relationship matters as much as the terms.
  • A deal that has stalled, hardened, or turned adversarial, and needs to be unlocked without losing face.
What you get
  • A written negotiation strategy: objectives, limits, alternatives, decision chain, scenarios.
  • Rehearsed responses to the moves the other side is most likely to make.
  • A cool head in the room when the pressure peaks.

Format
Workshops from one to three days, on site or remote, plus e-learning reinforcement
Group size
Eight to sixteen participants per session
Backed by
Halifax Consulting's international content and tooling
Who it's for
  • Sales teams facing professional buyers who are trained, incentivised, and patient.

The engagement method

How an engagement actually runs

Whatever the format, the sequence is the same, and it starts with evidence rather than opinion.

STEP 01

Diagnose

We look at real deals: where margin goes, who concedes what, and which habits repeat. No workshop before we know what we are fixing.

STEP 02

Design

The intervention is built backwards from the diagnosis: the right format, the right people, the right depth. Nothing generic.

STEP 03

Deploy

Method and practice, on your live files. Participants apply it the same week, with support when the first real test arrives.

STEP 04

Debrief

We measure against the indicators agreed at the start, and hand the method to internal relays so it survives without us.

Questions

Before you get in touch

How is this different from a generic negotiation course?

A course teaches a framework and sends you home. We start from your live files and stay with them. Every session ends with something you apply that week, and the engagement is measured against indicators you set (margin, discount rate, cycle length) rather than a satisfaction score.

Do you sit at the table with us?

Sometimes. In negotiation assistance, Nicolas can attend as an advisor or as a member of your delegation, but only when his presence helps your position. Often the stronger play is to prepare intensively and stay out of the room, so the other side reads no change in your posture.

What if our counterpart refuses any win-win approach?

That is one of the situations we are called in for most often. Collaborative technique alone does not survive a counterpart who has decided to be adversarial. The work then is about alternatives, information, timing and composure: holding a position without escalation, and making the cost of their stance visible to them.

Do you work outside Asia?

Yes. NegoAsia is based in the region and that is where most engagements happen, but the practice extends to Europe and to cross-border deals in either direction, which are frequently the hardest ones.

Which languages do you work in?

Engagements run in English and French. Sessions and materials can be delivered in either.

How confidential is this?

Completely. A mutual non-disclosure agreement is signed before any file, figure or name is shared, and client identities are never used as references without written permission.

What does an engagement cost?

It depends entirely on format and scope, so there is no price list. The free 30-minute audit exists precisely to size the problem first. After it, you receive a written proposal with a fixed scope and a fixed fee.

Not sure which one you need?

Describe the negotiation on your desk. Thirty minutes later you'll know where the value is leaking, and which format fixes it.
